Key Events

1830 The Hide Trade Begins

Commercial hunting for buffalo robes expands. For every robe that made it to market, several more buffalo were killed and left in the sun.

Isenberg, 2000
1860 Railroads Reach the Plains

Railroads enable mass slaughter. Hunters fired from train windows, leaving carcasses to rot.

Brown, 1970
1870 The Great Collapse

The population fell from millions to hundreds of thousands. Métis communities faced starvation.

Flores, 2016; Peterson, 1985
1874 US Army Campaigns

The strategy was simple: kill the buffalo, break the people. The U.S. Army destroyed the herds to force Indigenous nations onto reservations.

Congressional Globe, 1874
1883 The Last of the Herds

Fewer than 1,000 buffalo remained in the wild. The species was functionally extinct.

Hornaday, 1889
